== Description ==

Ury is a minimal blogging theme designed for comfortable, distraction-free reading. A fixed sidebar holds your site title, navigation, and tagline while your posts fill a wide content column. It offers a variety of highly readable typography pairings and three color schemes -- a light default, a warm sepia, and a dark mode -- so you can match the look to the mood of your writing.
